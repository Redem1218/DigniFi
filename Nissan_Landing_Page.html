The maximum approved amount, terms, and Annual Percentage Rate (APR) will be determined by an evaluation of your credit history. An origination fee of 4% applies. The DigniFi Interest Waived Program is available to all approved customers. Interest will be charged at an APR of 13.99% to 36.00% from account opening if the full loan amount is not paid within the first 90 days. A minimum monthly payment is required, and additional principal payments are needed to pay off the loan before the promotion expires. Interest will be waived if all scheduled payments are made on time and the loan is paid in full within the first 90 days.
