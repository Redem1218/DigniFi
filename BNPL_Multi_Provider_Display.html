How each touchpoint works, end to end — with more than one provider in play

A consumer can request Pay Over Time as early as scheduling — one neutral button, no provider names shown yet. The moment they tap it, the partner platform pings every enrolled provider at once. Each one decisions independently and, if approved, texts its own invite straight to the consumer — not through the partner's messaging rails. That gives the consumer 2 live offers, side by side, before they've even reached checkout. Tab 6 goes inside each provider's own hosted experience, the part that happens after the handoff.
