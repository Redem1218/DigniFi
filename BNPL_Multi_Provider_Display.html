How each Pay Over Time touchpoint works, end to end — with multiple providers

A consumer can request Pay Over Time as early as scheduling — no provider name shown, just one neutral CTA. The moment they tap it, the partner platform pings every enrolled financing provider at once. Each provider decisions independently, and if approved, sends its own pre‑qualification invite by text directly to the consumer — not through the partner's own messaging rails. That approval then follows the consumer through every touchpoint after, not just once at checkout. Pick a touchpoint below, then run the flow to see exactly what fires and when. The last tab shows what the consumer sees after tapping into their offers.

What the consumer sees after tapping a provider's text

Each provider sends its own invite text with its own link. Whichever link the consumer opens first — or a partner‑hosted summary page, if the integration includes one — brings together every offer that's come back so far into one side‑by‑side view.

1Consumer receives 1–2 separate texts, one per approving provider, each from that provider's own number.
2Opening either link resolves to the same shared view — every live offer tied to this repair order.
3Down payment is shown up front for every offer, since it changes what's actually due today.
4Selecting an offer routes the consumer into that provider's own checkout/signing flow.
